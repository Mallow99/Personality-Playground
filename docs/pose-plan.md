Reply with codes (for example, "keep S01–S04 and A01; revise H03") or use the live checklist and download your decisions. Shared means one concept adapted separately for each character. First four per group are recommended starting points, not approvals.

## Review and animation direction

Decide which silhouettes and gestures earn new artwork. Thinking, writing, listening, and speaking need distinct states, but a new application event need not require a new drawing. Talking can use voice marks, small gestures, and a readable speaking indicator. The current proof is silent and is not lip-sync.

Whole connected forms are being grouped intact for Academic standing/seated. Their face, hands, sleeves, book, and continuous legs travel as meaningful shapes. Joined forms are not automatically divided into anatomical joints. The rest of the library retains legacy region maps pending refinement. Pose changes offer turning contours, direct morphing, and a still comparison.
